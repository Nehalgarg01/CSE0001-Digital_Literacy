SOCIAL MEDIA DO'S AND DON'TS FOR COLLEGE STUDENTS


DO'S:
- Think before posting anything online.
- Maintain respectful communication.
- Protect personal information and privacy.
- Verify information before sharing.
- Use social media for learning and networking.


DON'TS:
- Do not share false information.
- Avoid posting inappropriate content.
- Do not overshare personal details.
- Avoid online arguments.
- Do not use disrespectful language.
