STAY SAFE ONLINE – PREVENTION CHECKLIST FOR COLLEGE STUDENTS


GENERAL ONLINE SAFETY : 
1. Use strong and unique passwords for every account.
2. Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) whenever available.
3. Think carefully before posting personal photos or information online.
4. Avoid clicking on suspicious links or downloading unknown files.
5. Keep your phone, apps, and antivirus software updated regularly.
6. Review privacy settings on social media accounts frequently.
7. Do not accept friend requests or messages from unknown people.
8. Avoid using public Wi-Fi for logging into important accounts.
9. Verify information before sharing posts to prevent spreading misinformation.
10. Block and report users who send abusive, threatening, or suspicious messages.


FINANCIAL / UPI SAFETY :
1. Never share OTP, UPI PIN, bank details, or passwords with anyone.
2. Remember: receiving money does NOT require entering your UPI PIN.
3. Do not approve unknown payment requests or collect requests.
4. Always verify payment links, QR codes, or refund calls before acting.


RESPONDING TO CYBERCRIME : 
1. Block and report abusive or suspicious accounts immediately. 
2. Save screenshots as evidence if harassment or fraud occurs.
